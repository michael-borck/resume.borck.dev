RESUME WORLD

A pixel-art overworld of a career

Welcome, traveller. This map holds the whole of Michael Borck's working life: every role, degree, tool, and triumph is a place to discover.

Explore with the arrow keys. When you reach a glowing landmark, press E to read its story. Clear all eight regions to complete the journey.

HOW TO PLAY

A field guide

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Walk close to a landmark and press E (or Enter) to open it. Press Esc to close a panel.

Regions light up on the tracker as you discover them. Click any region chip to jump straight to its story. On a phone, use the on-screen D-pad and the E button.
