# The Michael Borck Story
*A short conversation about a working life — teaching, building, and local AI.*

**Alex:** Welcome back. Today we're looking at Michael Borck — a career that runs from navy mainframes to a local-AI research lab. Sam, where do we start?

**Sam:** At the beginning. Back in Mar 1989 – Aug 1993, Michael was a System Administrator — Manpower and Personnel with RAN. That's the foundation: real systems, real consequences.

**Alex:** And then the turn — from the uniform to the whiteboard?

**Sam:** Exactly. A lecturer's post at Curtin University. Same instinct, different audience: explain the system so other people can use it.

**Alex:** There's a line he keeps coming back to in his teaching.

**Sam:** "Make Contact Count - maximising impact through every student interaction." It's not a slogan; it shows up in an 85% average student rating across 38 unit deliveries since 2019. The person is the point, not the platform.

**Alex:** And there's research underneath it.

**Sam:** A PhD in 2017 from Curtin University, on computer vision and feature extraction from mobile mapping data. That depth feeds the teaching.

**Alex:** Then the building — the tools.

**Sam:** Yes: Reality Reigns, Talk Buddy, Curriculum Curator, Feed Forward System. Swipe-based story games, AI conversation practice, curriculum tools, feedback systems. All aimed at keeping the human thinking.

**Alex:** And the books?

**Sam:** Four core books and a set of companions, self-published and free to read online. Wanting the work to travel matters more than owning it.

**Alex:** Which brings us to LocoLabo.

**Sam:** An applied local-AI lab: small models on modest hardware, keeping data on-premises. The thesis is conversation, not delegation.

**Alex:** Off the clock? I'm told Karate, Muay Thai, Krav Maga, Atari 2600 Programming.

**Sam:** Right — and it all connects. Discipline, curiosity, and a refusal to let the tool do the thinking.

**Alex:** That's Michael Borck. Reach him at michael.borck@curtin.edu.au. Thanks for listening.
