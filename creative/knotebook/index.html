The Valley

An Ink story of a working life

The same branching story from /creative/ink/, packaged as a Knotebook pack — a single .knotebook.json file with the compiled Ink story and all its artwork embedded.

Import it into the Knotebook player to read offline, keep progress, and switch themes.

  1. Download the pack below.
  2. Open the Knotebook player and choose Import pack.
  3. Pick the-valley.knotebook.json. It installs to your library.
